## What you get for free

You often don't need a button at all:

- **Labels** can be selected with the mouse and copied with Ctrl+C.
- **Text fields** support copy, cut and paste with the usual shortcuts.

## Good to know

- `ctx.copy_image(color_image)` copies a picture instead of text.
- Pasted text arrives as an input event, `egui::Event::Paste`. Text fields handle it themselves. To react to a paste elsewhere, look through `ui.input(|i| i.events.clone())` for it.
- Give feedback after copying, as the example does. Nothing visible happens otherwise, and people will click again.
